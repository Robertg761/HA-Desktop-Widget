# Sponsors

HA Desktop Widget is independently developed and available free and open source. Sponsorship helps support continued development, maintenance, cross-platform testing, development tools, infrastructure, hardware, services, and the time needed to keep the project moving forward.

Thank you to everyone who supports this work, financially or otherwise.

## Recognition

Recognition follows the benefit attached to each tier on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Robertg761).

- Eligible individual sponsors are listed here by GitHub username or preferred name.
- Eligible sponsors may also receive compact recognition in the README.
- Company sponsors may receive a website link or logo placement at qualifying levels.
- Monthly recognition remains while the sponsorship is active.
- Time-limited recognition for one-time sponsors ends after the period stated in that tier.
- Recognition is optional. Sponsors may remain anonymous.
- Links and logos must be appropriate for an open-source project.

Sponsor listings are reviewed periodically so this file stays accurate and manageable as the project grows.

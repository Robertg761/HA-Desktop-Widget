Cloud Sync subscription terms

Draft updated 30 September 2026 · Operator: Robert Gordon · robertgordon761@gmail.com

Planned service. This draft describes the proposed hosted Cloud Sync service. It does not describe features available in the 4.0 desktop app. Pricing, account controls, and availability may change before launch; final terms will be published when the service becomes available. The desktop app and free folder Profile Sync require no hosted account or subscription.

Trial and annual subscription

New Cloud Sync accounts can save settings for 14 days without a subscription or payment card. The planned paid subscription is USD 24 per year. The total, including any applicable tax, is displayed before you pay. Stripe handles payment and annual renewal. The 14 day service trial is separate from Stripe Checkout: if you subscribe during it, paid billing starts at checkout.

After the trial, saving changes requires an active subscription. If a subscription ends, you can still sign in and download the profile already stored. You can use the app's free folder sync or subscribe again.

Managing and cancelling

The planned Cloud Sync client would provide a Manage Subscription action opening Stripe's customer portal to update payment details and cancel. A portal cancellation would normally take effect at the end of the paid billing period, as shown in the portal. Account deletion would first attempt to cancel an active subscription before deleting stored account data. If cancellation failed, the account would remain in place for support to resolve it. These controls are not available in the 4.0 desktop app.

Refunds

We do not offer voluntary refunds. This does not limit any refund, cancellation, or other consumer right required by applicable law.

Your settings and access

You choose which settings to sync and are responsible for maintaining access to your Google or GitHub account. Individual profile uploads are limited to 512 KB. The service checks revisions to prevent one device from silently overwriting another device's newer save. Profile encryption is optional; without it, the service can read the synced profile.

Availability and changes

The service may occasionally be unavailable for maintenance or due to outages. We may change the service or the price for future billing periods after giving notice as required by applicable law.

Contact

For subscription or service questions, email robertgordon761@gmail.com. See our privacy policy for information about your data.

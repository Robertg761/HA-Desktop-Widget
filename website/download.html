How to install

Your download has started.

  1. Windows: run the installer, or double-click the portable .exe.
  2. macOS: open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications. The first time, Control-click it and choose Open.
  3. Linux: allow the AppImage to run as a program and double-click it, or install the .deb. Remembering your sign-in needs a running keyring, such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet.
  4. Enter your Home Assistant address and approve it in your browser.

Search everything

Press Ctrl+K to find any device, page or action. Global hotkeys control devices from any app.

Quick Access

Your devices in pages of tiles. Click to toggle, hold for more.

Desktop pins

Drop any tile onto your desktop as its own little window. On Omarchy, add tiles to the bar too.

Live cameras

Watch a live feed or refreshing snapshots without leaving the widget.

Light, dark and seasonal

Pick Auto, Dark or Light and any accent color. Holidays bring their own art, from jack-o'-lanterns to Santa's sleigh, and you can turn it off.

It never sees your password

You sign in on Home Assistant's own page, and the login is kept in your OS keychain.

Desktop alerts

Know when a door stays open, a reading crosses a line or a device drops off. Home Assistant's own notifications wait in the bell.

Same layout everywhere

Sync your pages and theme to your other computers, free through a folder or with Cloud Sync.

Running in about two minutes.

  1. 1

    Install and open

    Run the build for your OS and open the widget. A short setup walks you through the rest.

  2. 2

    Point it at Home Assistant

    Enter the address you use for Home Assistant and approve it in your browser.

  3. 3

    Add your entities

    Choose a room to start from, or click + and pick the lights, sensors and cameras you want.

Want automations to control it? Try the companion integration.

Your layout on every computer.

Sync your pages, tiles and theme between computers. Edits merge section by section, with a backup when two computers change the same thing. Sign-in tokens, desktop pins, window positions and hotkeys stay on each computer.

Folder sync

Free

  • Uses a folder you already sync, such as Dropbox or OneDrive
  • No account
  • Optional passphrase encryption

Cloud Sync

USD 24 a year

  • No sync app needed
  • Sign in with Google or GitHub
  • With encryption on, the service can't read your settings
  • 14-day free trial, no card needed

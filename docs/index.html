Connect to Home Assistant

Your password never enters this app.

Authorization happens in your own browser, the way Home Assistant intends, and comes back through a temporary loopback on your machine.

  1. Step 01

    Type the address you already use

    homeassistant.local, a local IP, or your own domain.

  2. Step 02

    Approve it in the browser

    Sign in where you always sign in. The widget picks the session up from there.

  3. Step 03

    Drag in what you care about

    Reorder, rename, re-icon, and pin the few you reach for most.
